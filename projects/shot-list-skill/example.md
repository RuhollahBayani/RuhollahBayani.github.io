# Example

**Brief given to Claude:**

> Perfume shoot for Mon Etoile Nº3 pour homme. Bottle on water, night-sky mood, stars. 2 hours in the studio. Need 6 finals for Instagram and one wide banner for the website.

**Plan Claude wrote with the shot-list skill:**

---

### Overview
A clear glass bottle sits on dark rippling water under a deep blue night sky, with a few stars added in post.
Mood: calm, cool, premium.
Deliverables: 6 Instagram images (4:5) and 1 website banner (16:9, 2400px wide).
Session: 2 hours in the studio.

### Assumptions
- One bottle, with a spare for spills and fingerprints.
- Stars and sky gradient are finished in Photoshop, not lit on set.
- A shallow black tray of water is available.

### Shot list

| # | Shot | Framing & lens | Light setup | Direction | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hero bottle on water, centred | Straight on, low angle, 100mm macro | A | Label square to camera, water still | Must |
| 2 | Hero with ripples | Same as 1 | A | Tap the tray off-frame between frames for rings | Must |
| 3 | Reflection focus | Low, 15° down, 100mm | A | Focus on the reflection, bottle soft | Nice |
| 4 | Wide for banner | Wide, bottle on right third, 50mm | B | Leave the left 60% empty for text | Must |
| 5 | Cap detail | Tight on cap, 100mm macro | C | Glass edges catch the strip light | Must |
| 6 | Label detail | Tight on label, 100mm macro | C | Gold foil angled to the light | Must |
| 7 | Spray mist | Medium, 85mm | C | Spray toward camera, backlit mist | Nice |
| 8 | Bottle on its side in water | Top-down, 50mm | B | Half submerged, ripples around | Nice |

### Light setups
- **A, hero:** Blue-gelled strip box behind and above the bottle for a rim; small softbox top front at 45° for the label; black flags on both sides to keep the glass edges dark. Background: dark blue paper, 2m behind.
- **B, wide:** As A, plus a second gelled light on the background for a gradient from dark at top to lighter at the horizon.
- **C, details:** One strip box at 90° to the side, white card fill opposite, background light off.

### Schedule
- 0:00-0:20 Set up tray, background and setup A
- 0:20-0:55 Shots 1-3
- 0:55-1:10 Change to B, shots 4 and 8
- 1:10-1:15 Clean and dry the bottle
- 1:15-1:45 Change to C, shots 5-7
- 1:45-2:00 Safety frames, back-up files, pack down

### Retouch & grade plan
- **Glass clean-up:** remove dust, water spots and fingerprints; rebuild clean glass edges where flags didn't fully work.
- **Grade:** cool blue shadows, slightly warmer highlights on the gold label, lifted blacks in the water.
- **Composite:** deepen the sky into a night gradient and add 3-5 soft star sparkles, never over the label.
- **Exports:** Instagram 1080 × 1350 (4:5); banner 2400 × 1350 (16:9).

### Gear checklist
- **Camera:** body, 100mm macro, 50mm, 85mm, tripod with an arm for top-down
- **Lighting:** 2 strip boxes, 1 small softbox, background light, blue gels, 2 black flags, white card
- **Grip:** C-stands, clamps, sandbags
- **Set:** black water tray, dark blue paper, microfibre cloths, gloves, spare bottle, spray bottle
- **Tech:** tether cable, laptop, 2 cards, charged batteries
